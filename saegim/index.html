A browser redrawing made to explain the process. The measuring step replays the nine contact points, the cylinder centre and radius from the 23 Sep 2026 real-robot record. The design is the crane-and-cloud motif that was really engraved on the candle, but the path drawn here is re-traced from the design image in the README and wrapped onto the cylinder; it is not the path file of a real run. The size and placement of the design, the arm and the fixture are illustrative. Acceptance limits and state names come from the repository. No physics, IK or force control runs here, and the real duration is compressed to about a minute.
